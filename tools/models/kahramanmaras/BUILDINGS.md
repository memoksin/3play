# Kahramanmaras building set (Fener, #123)

Neighbourhood pieces for 6 Feb 2023, the night between the two quakes. Style: `docs/art/style-2.5d.md`.
Build: `python3 tools/models/build.py bld_apartment bld_cutaway bld_pancake bld_rubble bld_shops bld_walls bld_house`
(~3.5 min, all 33). Look scene: `scenes/art_pilot/km_buildings.tscn` (one camera per row, `Hood` = a mixed street), regenerated by
`python3 tools/km_scene_gen.py` after adding pieces.

## Grid
- 1 unit = 1 m. Origin = footprint centre on the ground. Front = Godot +Z (Blender -Y). All four facades are dressed: rotate freely.
- Blocks: depth 9, width 9 / 12 / 15. Ground floor 3.4 m, upper floors 2.9 m. Put them edge to edge on one street line (0.3 m gap hides seams).
- Shops: 4.5 x 7, chain with no gap. Garden walls: 3 m modules, wall line on x (street side = +Z).
- Rubble/spill pieces overlap freely: drop `bld_rubble_spill` (12 x 4) in front of damaged blocks, `bld_rubble_s` between buildings.

## Lights to add in the level (models only carry unbaked emissive parts)
- `bld_shop_firin`: oven mouth glows (ember/fire). Add an OmniLight3D ~(0, 0.8, -1.5) local, warm 1.0/0.52/0.18, range 5: the bakery is a natural story light.
- `bld_apt_mint_4`, `bld_house_old`: one candle-lit window (window_glow). Small warm omni outside it if it should read in play.
- Cutaway rooms are dark on purpose: they are what the Fener torch reveals.
