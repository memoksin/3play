# Video script (1 min, MP4, mandatory) - DRAFT

Owner: burcubaran22 (issue #19). Rule: max 1 minute, MP4. Video language is open: narration is written in English and Turkish, pick one.
Only show what already works in the game. Black Saturday is Tier 2 and is not in this video unless it gets built.

## Idea for the pitch: four silent screens (to discuss with memoksin)

During the 3-minute pitch the jury plays, and the video runs behind us. Idea: instead of one video, show **four short clips side by side (a 2x2 wall), silent**, like four live streams. We talk over them with short lines, so the audience sees at once that there are different scenarios and different ways to play.

- Tiles: Kahramanmaras (Fit), Indian Ocean (Connect), solar flare 3026 (the easter egg). The fourth tile: the globe spinning, or Black Saturday if it is built.
- Each clip 5 to 8 seconds, looping, no sound (our voices are the audio).
- The one-minute video can stay a normal video; the wall is for the 3-minute pitch.
- How: record each scenario with screen capture, loop the clips in a video editor, play the 2x2 file behind the pitch.
- To decide with memoksin: who records the clips, and whether the wall is a video file or a Godot scene.

## Things to check before recording

- Record from a real Windows build, not the editor, in 1280x720.

- Music: the menu music (M1) can run under the whole video, quiet, once the audio files are in the game (`docs/audio.md`).
- Total length must be 60 seconds or less. Cut the narration, not the picture, if it runs long.
